# Welcome to STE-131 Management Science - Quantitative Analysis for Decision Making!

## Course topics and materials

This website serves as lecture notes throughout the course and provide complementary material in addition to the course textbook _An Introduction to Management Science: Quantitative APproaches to Decision Making_ by Anderson et al.

As the title of the course suggests, this course is all about the analysis of analysing data and processes and using the analysis as a basis for making decisions. This course consists of three broad themes:

1. Linear and mixed-integer optimization
2. Queue and inventory models
3. Forecasting and embedding statistical inference to decision making

The first part will take significant amount of time, and in the second part we will be using optimization techniques to derive key properties of the models through using tools from mathematical optimization. The last part will as well use the tools acquired thus far in the course either indirecly by giving interpretation to model parameters in statistical estimation, but also directly by parametrizing the optimization problem with the forecasting results [@janssen_assessing_2009].
